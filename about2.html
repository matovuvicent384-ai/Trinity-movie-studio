About Trinity Movie Studio & IT Centre


Trinity Movie Studio & IT Centre is a community-based media and information technology service centre located in Kiwatule, Balintuma Zone, Kampala.


The centre provides creative, media and technology-related services to customers. This website was developed as part of a community engagement project to improve how customers discover services, communicate with the centre and submit service requests online.


Our Purpose

To make professional media and IT services easier for customers to access and request.


Our Vision

To provide accessible, reliable and customer-focused media and technology services within the community.
